Hi! I am currently a PhD student in Economics at the Adam Smith Business School, University of Glasgow. I'm also the Lab Manager of Glasgow Experimental Lab (GaEL). My research focuses on behavioural and experimental economics, social networks, and subjective well-being. Passionate about understanding human behaviour, I aim to explore how individuals and social structures influence economic decision-making and overall well-being.
